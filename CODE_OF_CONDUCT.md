# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment. Be considerate, assume good intent while evaluating technical claims rigorously, protect the privacy and dignity of people represented by emergency data, and avoid using real personal or crisis information in demonstrations.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publishing private information, or knowingly exaggerating the system's safety guarantees. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
